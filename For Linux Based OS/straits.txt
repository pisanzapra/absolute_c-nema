Tired Straits 2: Brothers In Arms

Starring: Marc Knopfler, Johnny Illsley
-------------------------------
Genre: Drama, Action, War, History.
-------------------------------
Director: Reymond Chandler
-------------------------------

There's so many different worlds,
So many different suns.
And we have just one world.
But we live in different ones...

This is a sequel to the first movie called "Tired Straits: Telegraph Road".
It's about a soldier who is dying on the battlefield, surrounded by his comrades, who remain by his side as he slips away.
It's a look at the folly of war and the plight of those who fight them.

"♫ A long, long time ago came a man on a track.

Old Man:
